# EgoFalls playback examples

Published with explicit presentation-owner approval on 17 November 2026.

Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/vundle/egofalls-v2
Kaggle metadata: CC0: Public Domain. Dataset authorship: Xueyi Wang / EGOFALLS.

Fall: S_FI/S_FI/Indoor/falls/back_falls/Back_lying/Neck/S_FI_Back_lying_Neck_1.MOV
Non-fall: S_FI/S_FI/Indoor/nonfalls/Bending/bending/Neck/S_FI_bending_Neck_1.MOV

Both are real recordings of staged activities from participant S_FI, neck camera, indoor.

Media are transcoded to browser-compatible H.264/AAC MP4, 480x270, 15 fps. The full clip duration is retained. Audio is retained without intentional muting or normalization. JPEG images are extracted still frames at the indicated seconds.
