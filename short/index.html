From video to audio features.

Video → mono / 8 kHz → 5 s window → STFT → power → log-Mel / MFCC

\(E_k=\frac{1}{N}\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}x_k[n]^2\) · strongest positive change in 20 ms energy

Choose the window without class labels, before the STFT. A loud unrelated sound can attract the selector.

What remains before a reliable alert?

Broader evidence

Staged falls and few archive groups.
Confirm identity and related-event grouping.

Stable decisions

Validation settings transfer unevenly.
Study calibration on new development groups.

Independent test

Compare at similar achieved recall.
Measure real-world alarms over time.

Current result: offline clip classification. These experiments do not establish clinical performance or a validated deployment model.
